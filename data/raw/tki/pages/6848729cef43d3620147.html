27th JUNE 2024

12th InterAcademy Workshop: KAST/UniBern

On June 27th - 28th 2024 at the Gurten Park im Grünen, in Bern, the Theodor Kocher Institute co-organized the 12th InterAcademy Workshop: KAST/UniBern Networking Conference for Scientific Leaders with the topic Vascular and Neuroimmune Interactions. During this two-day workshop, several leading scientists from Asia, Europe and the United States presented research related to vascular biology, lymphatic clearance mechanisms of the central nervous system and meningeal biology. The event was co-organized by the Korean Academy of Science and Technology represented by Prof. Gou Young Koh and Prof. Aree Moon and the University of Bern represented by Prof. Britta Engelhardt and Dr. Steven Proulx.
