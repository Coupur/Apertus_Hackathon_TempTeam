Welcome to the inter-faculty focus "Neuroscience Bern" (former Clinical Neuroscience Bern)

Interdisciplinary collaboration is pivotal to success in neuroscience research and methodology. The research focus of Neuroscience Bern aims to improve information exchanges and promote cooperation with internal and external partners.

Here you will find interesting news and events as well as informations, links to other neuroscience related events and the Neurocience Bern Newsletter.

BRAIN WEEK BERN 2027

Die jährlich stattfindende Woche des Gehirns will das Verständnis der Öffentlichkeit für die Hirnforschung fördern. Koordiniert wird die Woche des Gehirns von der Europäischen Allianz für das Gehirn und der amerikanischen Dana Alliance for Brain Initiatives und hat sich zu einer bedeutenden internationalen Gemeinschaftsarbeit entwickelt.

Jeden März informieren weltweit hunderte von innovativen öffentlichen Veranstaltungen und Aktivitäten die breite Bevölkerung über die neusten wissenschaftlichen Erkenntnisse. An der Woche des Gehirns sind mehr als 1875 Partnerorganisationen beteiligt; unter anderem Schulen, Universitäten, Institutionen aus den Bereichen Medizin und Forschung, Patientenrechtgruppen, Regierungsstellen, Dienstleistungsbetriebe und Berufsverbände in 62 Ländern; sie alle orientieren über die Fortschritte in der Hirnforschung.

OTHER NEWS, ACTIVITIES AND INFOS

The NeuroClub

The NeuroClub is a student-led initiative that brings together curious minds from medicine, biomedical engineering, and AI to explore the fascinating world of neuroscience.

We aim to foster early interdisciplinary collaboration, promote academic exchange, and create a space where students and researchers connect across fields to learn, innovate, and grow together. Through talks, workshops, research opportunities, and social events, we build bridges between disciplines — and toward the future of brain science.

The NeuroClub

Stroke Research Center Bern (SRCB)

The Stroke Research Center Bern has been founded in 2020 at the University of Bern from the Departments of Neurology, Neurosurgery and the Institute of Diagnostic and Interventional Neuroradiology. Its goal is to provide a high-level environment to catalyze research and support young researchers in stroke medicine.

MAS in Stroke Medicine

The programme aims to train the next generation of clinicians and researchers in stroke care throughout Europe. An international faculty of world leading stroke experts will provide lectures, seminars and workshops in close collaboration with the local faculty. The programme offers an efficient mix of different learning methods, both in person und remote.

CAS in Brain Health

The CAS in Brain Health at the University of Bern introduces four key modules: Introduction to Brain Health, Brain Disorders and its Risk Factors, Brain Health Interventions, Brain Health Implementation, aiming to equip professionals with advanced skills in promoting and maintaining neurological health.

MAS in Sleep Medicine

The International Master in Sleep Medicine (MAS) - in collaboration with the University of Bern and the Università della Svizzera italiana as well as 14 international partner Universities - is a postgraduate master on sleep-wake-circadian physiology, consciousness and related disorders. This is an online program with two in-person seminars such as the Sleep Summer and the Sleep Winter School. In addition, a 2–4-week internship in a lab of the student’s choice or in one of our partner universities is included. The program begins every two years - the next cycle is planned to start in Spring 2027.
