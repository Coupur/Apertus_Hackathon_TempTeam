Continuing Education

Continuing Education at the Faculty

If you want to be successful in your job and have the latest knowledge, you should regularly attend further education courses. The Faculty of Sciences offers a variety of continuing education courses that provide you with practice-oriented knowledge at the cutting edge of research.
