News-Archive 2026

Gordon Bennett 2026 race

OCCR researcher supports succesful Swiss balloon pilots

OCCR PostDoc Killian Brennan contributed to the success of the Swiss gas ballooning pilots at the recent Gordon Bennett 2026 race.

Paleoecology

SNSF Grant for ECO-ICE

The Swiss National Science Foundation (SNSF) has awarded a grant of 3.5 million Swiss Francs to the OCCR led project ECO-ICE.

Urban Climate

Strong Turnout at the “Beat the Heat” Conference

Around 120 people gathered at the University of Bern to address the challenges of urban heat. The “Beat the Heat 2026” conference was organized by the OCCR.

New paper

Putting Switzerland’s 2026 Drought into Perspective

OCCR researchers have compared droughts in the 21st century with those of the past 600 years. Their findings show that the risk of drought is increasing sharply as climate change progresses.

Media release (in German and French)

Antarctica’s Coastal Blind Spots Complicate Sea-Level Projections

A large-scale international study involving OCCR researcher Julien Bodart highlights major gaps in our understanding of processes along Antarctica’s coast. These gaps are among the biggest obstacles to reliably projecting future global sea-level rise.

Elite Sports and University

First a Master’s Degree, Then the Olympics

Angeline Favre needs the ability to stay focused both on the fencing piste and in her studies. As an épée fencer on the Swiss national team and a climate sciences student, she navigates two worlds that both demand her full attention.

Funding

Inno Grant for OCCR reseacher

Patrick Kallabis has been awarded a UniBE Inno Grant for myurbanclimate, a web-based planning tool for standardized urban temperature monitoring networks.

New paper

A tale of two hot spells

In a new paper, OCCR researchers examine the atmospheric dynamics that support long‑lasting hot spells in Western Europe. According to the authors, the atmospheric flow underlying persistent hot spells can be grouped into two characteristic ways the system can set up these events.

Promotion

Gunter Stober Promoted to Associate Professor

The Executive Board of the University of Bern has promoted OCCR member Gunter Stober from postdoctoral researcher to associate professor.

PAGES

Researchers gather for inaugural IMPACT workshop

From 11–13 May 2026, researchers from around the world gathered at the University of Bern for the inaugural workshop of the new PAGES working group IMPACT. The workshop was hosted by OCCR researcher Frerk Pöppelmeier.

NAWDIC campaign

The Cloud Chaser

OCCR researcher Franziska Aemisegger spent hours flying in a plane beneath the clouds over the North Atlantic. In this profile, she explains what she hopes to discover and why she owes her research career to a famous French author.

Media release

Laura Mai is endowed professor for climate law and society

The Executive Board of the University of Bern has appointed Dr. Laura Mai to the endowed professorship for climate law and society. She will assume her position on 1 August 2026.

NAWDIC campaign

A field trip into the storm

OCCR student Patricia Gribi joined the NAWDIC international measurement campaign in February 2026. This is how she experienced her first field trip.

UrbanLab Viererfeld (in German)

Turning a new neighborhood into a lab

Within 20 years, 3,000 people are expected to live in Bern’s Viererfeld neighborhood. Several OCCR researchers participate a number of research projects to monitor the transformation of this land reserve into a new neighborhood.

Start of NCCR Clim+

Change in OCCR presidency

As Karin Ingold has taken over as Co-Director of the new NCCR Clim+, she stepped down as OCCR President. She was replaced by Vice President Stefan Brönnimann.

Swiss Global Change Day 2026

OCCR PhD students win poster awards

This year’s Swiss Global Change Day saw a big success for our researchers: At the event’s poster contest, three out of the four award-winning posters were created by OCCR members.

Media release

Succulent role models: opportunities for plants under drought

OCCR researchers Heike Lindner and Michael Raissig have discovered how a small succulent regulates the uptake of carbon dioxide while conserving water. The findings might be translated to crops to induce higher drought resistance and ensure yields during heat and drought.

ClimeApp

OCCR researchers create app for paleoclimate scientists and historians

After two years of development, a team of OCCR researchers has completed the ClimeApp, a web-based tool for processing and visualising climate data.

Urban Climate

Climate researcher with a background in craftsmanship

As a climate scientist at the OCCR, Patrick Bigler has developed a mobile measuring tower to enable more precise measurements of Bern's urban climate. And as a trained metalworker, he also constructed the device himself.

PAGES

OCCR contributes to new working groups

Researchers affiliated with the OCCR are contributing to four newly launched PAGES working groups. The initiatives support coordinated international research on past climate change, advancing shared methods, data integration, and interdisciplinary collaboration across the global paleoclimate community.

Media release

Karin Ingold is co-head of NCCR Clim+

The University of Bern is taking over the direction of a new National Centre of Competence in Research (NCCR) together with ETH Zurich.
