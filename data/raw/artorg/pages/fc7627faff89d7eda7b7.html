Student Projects

We are always interested in hosting BSc and MSc projects. Feel free to contact Prof. Dr. Raphael Sznitman (raphael.sznitman@unibe.ch) for more information or your own ideas for projects (please send your CV and course grades as well).

In general, our projects revolve around developing new and creating computer vision and machine learning approaches for clinical applications. As such, a background in image processing, machine learning or other related domains is typically desired. Knowledge in some form of programming language is typically preferred.

Here is a list of projects currently offered :

Adapting Vision-Language Models to MRI Data (PDF, 940KB)
Automated Slide Metadata Extraction and Tissue Classification from Polarimetry Data (PDF, 195KB)
Design of Automated Optical Testing Systems for Polarimetry Devices (PDF, 202KB)
Machine-Learning-Based prediction of Dry-to-Wet AMD conversion (PDF, 344KB)
Spectral Optimization of Polarimetry Based Cancer Diagnostics (PDF, 196KB)
3D Generative Models for Multi-Parametric MRI (PDF, 945KB)
