Education and Academic Career

Education and Academic Career:

  • 2009: Venia docendi for neurology
  • 04/07 - today: Senior consultant  in the Division for Cognitive and Restorative Neurology, Inselspital, Bern University Hospital, Prof. Dr. R. Müri
  • 04/06 – 03/07: Research associate, INSERM U679, Prof. C. Pierrot-Deseilligny, Hôpital de la Salpêtrière, 75651 Paris cedex 13, France (supported by Swiss Foundation for Grants in Biology and Medicine)
  • 01/03 – 03/06: Senior consultant in Neuropsychological Rehabilitation, Department of Neurology, Inselspital Bern, Prof. Dr. R. Müri
  • 10/04: FMH-title for Neurology
  • 08.11.2002: Certification for Neurosonology
  • 17.10.2002: Member of the Swiss Society for Neuroscience
  • 01/02 – today: Research associate, Perception and Eye Movement Laboratory, Department of Clinical Research, Prof. Dr. R. Müri*
  • 24.3.2001: Member of the Swiss Society for Behavioral Neurology
  • 01/01 – 12/02: Clinical formation in Neurology, Department of Neurology, Inselspital Bern, Prof. Dr. med. C.W. Hess
  • 10/99 – 09/00: Clinical formation in Neurorehabilitation, Department of Neurology, University Hospital Geneva, Prof. Dr. med. A. Schnider
  • 10/98 – 09/99: Clinical formation in Neurology, Department of Neurology, University Hospital Geneva, Prof. Dr. med. T. Landis
