CAS Sustainability Transformation

The previously existing CAS programme in Sustainable Development is being redesigned as a new, innovative and practice-oriented CAS programme in Sustainability Transformation.

The new CAS course in Sustainability Transformation will start in spring 2027 with the motto ‘Transformation – shaping a sustainable future’. The previously existing CAS programme in Sustainable Development will no longer be offered in its previous form.

CDE strives to continue and expand its pioneering role in sustainability research and education. Amidst the current challenges, we are all the more committed to developing an educational programme that teaches the skills needed to overcome the current crises and shape a sustainable future. In the new programme, we will go beyond diagnostic approaches, present scientifically based development paths towards sustainability, work together to develop key practical skills for actively shaping and leading sustainability transformations, delve deeper into topics in a needs-oriented manner, and build a committed and solution-oriented ‘community of practice’ together.

We would be delighted if you would like to work with us towards this goal. Detailed information on the new CAS programme in Sustainability Transformation can be found on the following website and is being updated on an ongoing basis.
