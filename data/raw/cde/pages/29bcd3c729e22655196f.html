How can we shrink our trade-related footprint?

With a range of new trade rules, the EU is hoping to reduce the global harms of its imports, including deforestation, pollution, human rights violations, etc. But what will this mean for producer countries, smallholders, and other vulnerable groups? And how could the new rules be improved to address their concerns? Below, you'll find relevant information and inspiring ideas.

Can the giants be tamed?

Despite innovations and improvements in efficiency, mainstream development still demands massive amounts of steel, concrete, and precious elements. And what about the effects on local communities and nature? Our research chronicles the impacts of these activities and offers guidance on ways to improve.
