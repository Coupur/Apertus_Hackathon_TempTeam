Accommodation

We have prebooked a number of rooms in reasonably priced hotels close to the University of Bern. The rooms are reserved until end of May -- after that, availability will gradually decrease. Participants are asked to book their accommodation through the official booking site www.bern-incoming.ch.


Student housing at a cost of 50-60 CHF/night can be reserved through www.vbsl.ch. A block of rooms is reserved until end of May. Please mention on your application for a room that you are a XQCD13 participant. If you request student support on the registration page, we may be able to cover part of the housing expenses.
