Welcome to the homepage of Matthias Blau




  • Professor for Theoretical Physics at the Institute for Theoretical Physics and the Albert Einstein Center for Fundamental Physics of the University of Bern, and Head of the Gravity and String Theory Group.

  • Physics Representative (and formerly Vice-President and acting President) of the Studienausschuss of the Faculty of Science.



    • Research Interests

    My research interests include, in general,
    • String Theory and Quantum Gravity
    • General Relativity
    • Topological Field Theory
    • Geometrical and Topological Aspects of QFT
