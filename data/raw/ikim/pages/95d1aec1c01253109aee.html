Welcome to IKIM

The Institute for Complementary and Integrative Medicine IKIM combines conventional medicine and complementary medicine in the sense of integrative medicine.

We actively combine research, teaching and clinical activity. In this way, we contribute to the further development of experience and knowledge in medicine and pharmacy and thus also to the improvement of therapeutic options for patients.

We work on an interdisciplinary and interprofessional basis in both, our research and clinical activities.
