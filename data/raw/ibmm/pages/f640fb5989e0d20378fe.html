Curriculum Vitae

Angelo AZZI, born 26 October 1939, Modena, Italy.

DEGREES
MD 1963, University of Padua
PhD General Pathology, 1969 University of Padua
PhD Biochemistry, 1970 University of Padua

POSITIONS
1963-1966 Assistant Professor, University of Padua
1967-1969 Res. Fellow, Department of Biophysics Uni. of Pennsylvania
1970-1975 Associate Professor, University of Padua
1976-1977 Full Professor, University of Padua
1977-1984 Professor & Head Department Medical Chemistry Institute, Uni. Bern
1984-2005 Professor and Director, Inst. Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, Uni. Bern
2005-2009 Visiting Scientist HNRCA
2009-2014 Senior Scientist HNRCA

PRESENT EDITORIAL ACTIVITIES:
Editor-in-Chief of Molecular Aspects of Medicine
Editor-in-Chief of IUBMB Life
Review Editor BioFactors
Editorial board Free Radical Research
Editorial board Genes & Nutrition
Associate Editor Mechanisms of Ageing and Development (from Jan 2015)

PRESENT MEMBERSHIP IN INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS
President International Union of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology (2006-2012)
Past-President IUBMB (2013-)
Member Scientific Council -Warsaw Int. Institute of Cell and Molecular Biology (1997-)
Board Member, Institute of Genetic Chemistry and Laboratory Medicine, Nigeria,
Past-President Society for Free Radical Research International (2010-)
Member of Advisory Board of UNESCO (ISAB/IBSP) (1997-)
Member Advisory Committee, Regional Center for Biotechnology, Delhi (2010-)
Member Advisory Committee, Tripoli International Biotechnology Center (Libya) (2005-)
