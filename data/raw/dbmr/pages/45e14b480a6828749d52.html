The Day of BioMedical Research

Information

For more information please contact:

E-Mail-Address
dayofbiomedicalresearch.dbmr@unibe.ch

Since 1996, the Department for BioMedical Research (DBMR) has held a Day of BioMedical Research. The annual Day of BioMedical Research has established itself as a forum for researchers to present their work in a poster session and to get insights into the research projects of their colleagues. The event is open to the public, and gives an insight into the clinical and translational research activities at the University of Bern. The Day of BioMedical Research is also an excellent platform for making new contacts and exchanging ideas.

DBMR Day of BioMedical Research 2026

The DBMR Day of BioMedical Research was held on Wednesday, July 1, 2026. The Program included a lecture by the keynote speaker, Prof. Mark A. Rubin MD, and the award ceremony of several prizes (details below).

Awards Ceremony:

The following prizes were awarded at the DBMR Day of BioMedical Research 2026:

  • Johanna Dürmüller-Bol DBMR Research Award 2026 by the Foundation Johanna Dürmüller-Bol. Submission open until Thursday, March 26, 2026.
  • Best DBMR Publication 2025 Prize (CHF 1'000). Submission open until March 19, 2026.
  • Benoît Pochon Prize 2025 (CHF 1'000). Submission open until March 10, 2026.
  • DBMR Technician of the Year 2026 (CHF 1'000). Submission open until March 2, 2026.
  • Poster Prizes to young researchers. Abstract submissions will open Friday, February 20, 2026 and will be open until Monday, April 19, 2026.
    • DBMR Poster prize for the Best Preclincal Project (CHF 2'000)
    • DBMR Poster Prize for the Best Clinical Project (CHF 2'000)
    • DBMR Poster Prize for the Best Project by a Medical Student (CHF 2'000)
    • Research Prize Alumni MedBern (CHF 2'000)

Congratulations to all the awardees:

Poster Prizes of the Day of BioMedical Research 2026

  • Anja Helmer received the the prize for the Best Preclinical Project for the poster entitled
    Sex differences in cardiac recovery and ventricular gene expression in a rat model of donation after circulatory death (DCD)
  • Lara Marie Aschenbrenner received the the prize for the Best Clinical Project for the poster entitled
    Internal Control Bias Related to Death Implicitly Differs in Suicidal Behaviour based on Frequency and Recency of Attempts
  • Kai Busslinger received the the prize for the Best Project by a medical student for the poster entitled
    Cardiac Arrythmia Detection using Photoplethysmography in Real-world Ambulatory Setting
  • Melanie Scalise received the Alumni MedBern Research Award for the poster entitled
    Host-associated microbial evolution generates dominant gut communities that reshape metabolism
Benoît Pochon Prize 2025
  • Saranda Nimani, QT syndromes: arrhythmogenic mechanisms and novel therapeutic approaches
Prize for the Best DBMR Publication 2025
  • Chrysanthi Kouri, Oligogenic analysis across broad phenotypes of 46,XY differences in sex development associated with NR5A1/SF-1 variants: findings from the international SF1next study
DBMR Technician of 2026
  • Matthias Brunner, Lung Precision Medicine
