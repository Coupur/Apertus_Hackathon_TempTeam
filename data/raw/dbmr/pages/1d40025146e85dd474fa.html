Prize for Best DBMR Publication

The Prize for Best DBMR Publication recognizes an outstanding publication led by a young researcher from the DBMR. The awardee must have listed the DBMR as an affiliation and be the first author of the paper. Master students, PhD students, and postdoctoral researchers who are listed as first author are strongly encouraged to apply. The prize amounts to CHF 1'000 (before taxes) to be freely used by the awardee.

For further details and questions, please contact us.

The call for submissions for the Best DBMR Publication 2026 will open in February 2027.
