Plant Sciences / Botanical Garden

Reachable with public transports

  • BERNMOBIL bus line 20 direction Wankdorf Bahnhof, stop Gewerbeschule
Show larger map

For pedestrians, in 10 minutes from Bern railway station. Follow "Bollwerk" in a northern direction. Then cross the River Aare on the “Lorrainebrücke” and use the entrance to the Botanical Garden on the right-hand side of the bridge.
