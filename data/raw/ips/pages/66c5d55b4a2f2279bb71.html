Documentary About Open Access

  • Paywall: The Business of Scholarship

The world of academic publishing is all about credibility, and most scholars want to be published in the most reputable academic journals. But accessing those journals can be very expensive. One possible solution is open-access publishing. But the move to open-access is complicated, and the closed business model has helped some academic publishers achieve higher profit margins than Apple or Google.
