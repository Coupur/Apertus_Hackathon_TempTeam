Lorena Ramirez Gonzales

Cereal Cultivation in Arid Regions

In the BOOSTER Project, Lorena Ramirez Gonzales is part of the team that deals with genetic and biostimulatory aspects and is responsible for experimental design and data analysis. She is working to make maize and teff more drought-tolerant. In the interview, she explains how her work is helping to stabilize agriculture in arid regions and improve food security.

Das Online-Magazin der Universität Bern (in German)

Initiative Afrique

Establishing a Link Between Africa and Europe

Prof. Zerihun Tadele, group leader in Crop Breeding and Genomics at the IPS Bern, is currently working on a HORIZON project called "Booster" involving 12 countries in Africa and Europe. The project focuses on improving the drought tolerance of maize and tef and, at the same time, promotes research partnership between African and European scientists.
