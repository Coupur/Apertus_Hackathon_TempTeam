Research at a glance

Geology

Human activities can increase the risk of tsunamis in lakes

A study involving the University of Bern shows how human land use over the centuries can increase the risk of tsunamis in large lakes. Increased soil erosion destabilizes underwater slopes – with consequences for inhabited lakeshores.

To the media release (in german)

Universe

Exotic methanol discovered in planet-forming regions

An international research team co-led by the University of Bern has now demonstrated, through the large-scale COMPASS project at the ALMA Observatory, that young, Sun-like stars are surrounded by a surprisingly rich “chemical starter kit”.

Successful companies build on their strength

A study led by Jonathan Matzinger of the University of Bern shows that companies with pronounced strengths can be more successful than firms that are rated equally well across all areas.
