From Research Group to Founding Team

Dienstag, 17.11.2026, 12:00 Uhr

Founding teams rarely break over the technology. They break over things nobody said out loud early. Where the company is going. Who decides. In a lab spin-off, the research-group hierarchy and licensing negotiations add to the already long list of important tasks to master. This webinar covers essential strategies to build a strong team and the decisions that lock in alignment: the equity split, vesting, how the team makes decisions, and who commits to what. The INNO.Info.Bites are a bite-sized lunch webinar series organized by the Innovation Office.
