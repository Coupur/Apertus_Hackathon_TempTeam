Studies

Detailed informations about courses, practicals, seminars, lecture series, excursions and teachers can be found in KSL (Core Teaching System). This site provides only a short description of their contents and brief related instructions.

Teaching plans for the first three years (BSc in Biology) and for the MSc in Ecology and Evolution are provided on the website of the Institute of Ecology and Evolution.

Course registration and further details are found within the ILIAS (Zentrale E-Learning Plattform) system.

IMPORTANT: If you want to enrol for a BSc or MSc in Conservation Biology, you must take part before the start of the third year to our field and lab practical which takes place in Valais and Bern in July/August (field part) and in September/October (lab part).

The programme of our seminars and the journal club are found under Seminars & events
