About Us

General Secretariat

Responsibilities:

Dissertations, habilitations, professorships, finances, evaluations, examination fees

Opening hours:

Monday - Thursday 09.00 - 11.30

Bachelor's and Master's students should contact the Dean's Office of Student Affairs located at Murtenstrasse 11.

Phone:

+41 31 684 00 50

The telephone is staffed during the following hours: Monday to Thursday 09.00 - 12.00 and 14.00 - 16.00

E-Mail:

Outside opening hours and to make an appointment you can reach us via: info.meddek@unibe.ch

Mailing Address:

Universität Bern
Medizinische Fakultät
Dekanat
Murtenstrasse 24

Student Affairs

Responsibilities:

Bachelor and Master studies

Appointment Booking:

There are no general counter hours. Student inquiries can only be discussed by prior appointment via e-mail to studium.meddek@unibe.ch.

The Dean’s Office of Student Affairs is located at Murtenstrasse 11.

Letterbox:

The "Studiendekanat" letterbox is accessible from Monday to Friday from 07:30 - 18:00.

However, on the day before a public holiday, the mailbox is only accessible until 12:00 noon.

Mailing Address:

Medizinische Fakultät
Studiendekanat
