Faculty of Medicine

We train nearly 2,500 students in human medicine and dentistry, making us one of the largest medical faculties in Switzerland. A strong emphasis on practical relevance in our degree programs, an exceptional diversity of research topics, and a high level of innovation characterize our faculty. Close links between basic research, the clinics, and engineering sciences create the optimal conditions for cutting-edge medical research and translation.

Our Research

Four research routes to a healthier future

How does the Faculty of Medicine contribute to the fight against common diseases? Through four examples, researchers demonstrate how their work is improving the treatment of dementia, cancer, mental health conditions and obesity, thereby benefiting the general population

Novel learning mechanism for brain-inspired networks

An international team led by the NeuroTMA research group at the Institute of Physiology has developed a novel learning mechanism for neural networks modeled after the human brain: “Spike-based Alignment Learning”. Picture: Heidelberg University

Media Release

Showing emotions makes physicians more trustworthy

When physicians show their own emotions or share relevant personal experiences during conversations about serious illnesses, they are perceived by the Swiss public as more trustworthy and compassionate – without compromising their professionalism. This is shown by a new study from the University of Bern involving over 1,500 participants.

Media Release

Fecal microbiota transplantation: Right bacterial strain counts, not diversity

A new study from the University of Bern and Inselspital shows: Whether a fecal microbiota transplantation is effective does not depend on the quantity of “good” bacteria or the diversity of the transferred gut bacteria. Rather, the key factor is whether the right strains can colonize the gut and displace existing ones.

Our People

New research group in Translational Cancer Research

Anke Augspach has been appointed Assistant Professor after securing competitive third-party funding. She has joined the Translational Cancer Research Program at the Department for BioMedical Research DBMR. Her lab focuses on the minor spliceosome, a unique RNA splicing machinery with elevated activity in cancer.

Claudio Bassetti to become Rector of the Università della Svizzera italiana

In September 2026, Claudio Bassetti, Dean of our Faculty and Director Teaching and Research at Inselspital, Bern University Hospital, will take up the position of Rector at the Università della Svizzera italiana (USI). We would like to thank him for his many years of very successful service to the Faculty and wish him every success in his new role.
