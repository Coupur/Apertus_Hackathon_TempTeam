University Comprehensive Cancer Center Inselspital

Twelve organ-specific cancer centers form the core of the UCI – supported by interdisciplinary services and expert groups that focus on specific aspects of cancer care. The UCI is embedded in the Faculty of Medicine and the Inselspital Bern. It includes the Cancer Research Network Bern (CRNB), the research arm of the UCI.

To the UCI website

Chair and Member of the Board of Directors

Prof. Daniel M. Aebersold

Profile

  • The UCI aims to establish the best possible prevention, diagnostics, therapy, aftercare, and rehabilitation for cancer patients. This requires specialist and interdisciplinary cooperation between all institutions involved in tumor treatment.
  • The organs of the UCI support the coordination of patient care according to the latest scientific knowledge.
  • Particular importance is attached to the establishment of interdisciplinary, cross-institutional care and the implementation of quality assurance measures.

Highlights 2025

NETCARE network: a Europe-wide collaboration in cancer research and education

We are delighted that our initiative, NeTwork for CAncer Research and Education (NETCARE), bringing together all ten ENLIGHT partner universities and encompassing over 150 cancer research teams, is being funded by ENLIGHT. The initiative strengthens joint efforts to further advance cancer prevention, diagnosis, treatment and patient care in Europe. It fosters collaboration among scientists, clinicians and educators to share expertise, cutting-edge research technologies and best practices. NETCARE promotes innovation, supports young researchers through exchange and training opportunities, and drives the development of new research programs and therapies. By connecting both emerging and established cancer centres, the network creates a dynamic ecosystem with the critical mass and diversity needed for impactful research and enriched education. NETCARE also prepares a new generation of scientists to tackle the future challenges of cancer.

UCI celebrates premiere at Night of Research

The festival ‘Night of Research’ on 6 September gave the people of Bern a fascinating insight into the research being carried out at their university. It enabled personal encounters between researchers and the public. The UCI took part for the first time, represented by the Cancer Research Network Bern (CRNB).

Optimising the treatment of eye tumours

The Insel Group and the Paul Scherrer Institute PSI are expanding their collaboration to provide faster and better coordinated access to proton therapy for patients with eye tumours. The two institutions have signed a new cooperation agreement, creating an integrated patient pathway that offers patients medically coordinated, high-precision treatment and improves the long-term quality of the care they receive.

The agreement combines the strengths of two leading institutions: PSI as one of the internationally most renowned centres for proton therapy and Inselspital, whose University Comprehensive Cancer Center Inselspital (UCI) plays a central role in ocular oncology throughout Switzerland.
