A number of support options are available to researchers at the Faculty of Medicine, both in financial and other forms. The support programs differ depending on the academic career stage of the person.

DBMR Program Call

Deadline for submission is December 15, 2026

As part of its strategic development, the Department for BioMedical Research (DBMR) is implementing a new program structure and invites PIs to establish research programs.

More information

Talent4Bern Program

Deadline for submission is November 1, 2026

The fifth call for the Talent4Bern program is now open. The program offers support in applying for a 2027 SNSF Starting Grant - and more!

More information

GRANTS FOR VISITING SCHOLARS FROM UKRAINE

The call is open and has a rolling deadline.

The Faculty of Medicine offers grants to visiting scholars (professors and post-doctoral researchers) from Ukraine to teach and/or conduct research at the Faculty of Medicine.

More information

University of Bern Grants Office and Euresearch Office

The Grants Office was established to optimize and simplify the process of acquisition of third-party funding for researchers at the University of Bern. It is the primary point of contact for questions related to competitive research funding programs and international grants. The Grants Office is part of the Vice-Rectorate Research and runs the Euresearch Office University of Bern.
