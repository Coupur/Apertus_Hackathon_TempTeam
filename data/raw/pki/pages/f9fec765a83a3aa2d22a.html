Welcome to the Institute of Pharmacology (PKI) – a place of excellence for innovative research and outstanding teaching

Our research groups investigate the pharmacology of cancer and inflammation. Using state-of-the-art methods, we develop new therapeutic approaches and treatment options. Our spectrum ranges from basic research on drugs and their mechanisms of action to clinical studies in humans.
At the University of Bern, we contribute to education in pharmacology and immunology for students of medicine, pharmacy, dentistry, biomedicine, and biology. A key focus of our institute is the training and supervision of PhD students.
Join us in shaping the future of pharmacology – we look forward to your curiosity and passion for discovery!
