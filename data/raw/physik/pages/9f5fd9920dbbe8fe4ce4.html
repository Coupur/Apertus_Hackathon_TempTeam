Bachelor's program

Please note that the Bachelor's program is taught in German.

Major Physics (120 ECTS) and Minor Mathematics (30 ECTS)

The ECTS credits for each course are indicated in parentheses.

1. Semester

  • Crash Course Physics I & II (0)
  • Math. Methods in Physics I (4.5)
  • Physics I (6.5)
  • Analysis I (8)
  • Linear Algebra I (8)

2. Semester

  • Math. Methods in Physics II (4.5)
  • Mechanics I (6)
  • Physics II (6.5)
  • Practical Course I (4)
  • Proseminar I (1)
  • Analysis II (7)
  • Linear Algebra II (7)

3. Semester

  • Electrodynamics (4)
  • Math. Methods in Physics III (4)
  • Introduction to Programming and Computational Physics (4)
  • Practical Course II (4)
  • Quantum Theory I (4)

4. Semester

  • Atomic Physics (4)
  • Numerical Methods in Physics (5)
  • Mechanics II (4)
  • Quantum Theory II (4)
  • Statistical Thermodynamics I (4)

5. Semester

  • Solid-State Physics (4)
  • Nuclear Physics (2)
  • Laboratory Course I (10)
  • Quantum Theory III (4)
  • Statistical Thermodynamics II (4)

6. Semester

  • Bachelor's Thesis (10)
  • Elementary Particle Physics (4)
  • Classical Field Theory (4)
  • Optics (4)

Minors in Physics and Astronomy

Physics Minor programs at the Bachelor's level are offered for 60, 30, and 15 ECTS credits. In addition a Bachelor's Minor in Astronomy is available with 30 and 15 ECTS credits. Detailed information on the composition of the learning units for these programs can be found in the German-language Study plan.
