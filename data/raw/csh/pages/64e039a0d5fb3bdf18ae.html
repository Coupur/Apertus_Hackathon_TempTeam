At the Center for Space and Habitability (CSH) we offer events for researchers as well as outreach and educational activities for the public. Together with our partners and the space research community at the University of Bern, the CSH seeks to foster dialogue and interactions between various scientific disciplines, and aims to make space research accessible to all audiences.

For school visits, please contact Sophie Krummenacher.
