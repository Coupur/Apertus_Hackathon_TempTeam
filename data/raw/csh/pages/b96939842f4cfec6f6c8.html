Fostering interdisciplinary research across Earth and planetary sciences

Established in 2011, the Center for Space and Habitability (CSH) fosters interdisciplinary research across Earth and planetary sciences, the search for life in our Universe, philosophy of science, biomedical research and more. The CSH connects research groups located at different institutes and faculties of the University of Bern to trigger curiosity-driven research and cross-fertilization of ideas.

Mission Highlight

The JUICE Mission — Jupiter's Icy Moons Explorer

On September 28, JUICE returns to Earth for a flyby maneuver where flight controllers will guide the spacecraft close to our home planet to alter its speed and direction en route to Jupiter. Follow along here and keep up to date with JUICE.

Announcements

Ready to discover new worlds? Planet Hunters CHEOPS is live!

ESA launched Planet Hunters CHEOPS, a new citizen science project that invites the public to help search for exoplanets using data from the CHEOPS space telescope, which was developed at, and is managed by, the University of Bern.

CSH & WP’s Space Community visits Empa for Annual Excursion

In an annual tradition, the Center for Space and Habitability (CSH) and the Space Research and Planetary Sciences Division (WP) at the University of Bern, took an educational field trip. This year, the excursion took us to the Empa in Dübendorf.
