CSH and WP’s Space Community visits Empa for Annual Excursion

In an annual tradition, the space community at the University of Bern, made up of the Center for Space and Habitability (CSH) and the Space Research and Planetary Sciences Division (WP), took an educational field trip. This year, the excursion took us to the Swiss Federal Laboratories for Materials Science and Technology (Empa) in Dübendorf.

On August 24, 2026, the traditional annual “Bildungsausflug” took the space community at CSH and WP to the Empa facilities in Dübendorf for an afternoon of research exchange and networking.

Empa, the Swiss Federal Laboratories for Materials Science and Technology, is dedicated to the development and use of materials, innovative technologies and resilient solutions for a sustainable future. Employing roughly 1000 scientists, engineers, technicians and general staff, Empa is a world-renowned research institute engaged in research and development in four research focus areas: Nanoscale Materials and Manufacturing Technologies; Built Environment; Health; and Energy, Resources and Emissions.

For our visit, Empa prepared insights into what interests us most: space research. Throughout the afternoon, we had the pleasure to hear from various Empa members about their work—from inputs on the institution’s overall purpose and activities as well as insights into space related collaborations, to poster presentations and laboratory tours on sustainability robotics, Empa’s durability lab, inflatable multilayer space structures, joining for space applications, X-ray analytics and innovative materials for metal 3D-printing in space applications.

Following these fascinating presentations and conversations, the members of our space community were able to enjoy a lovely apéro, network with our hosts, and exchange valuable insights about research and development, academic publication, and science communication and outreach in the space sector.

On behalf of the space community at the University of Bern, we would like to thank Prof. Matthias Sulzer, Dr. Lorenz Herrmann, Dr. Bastian Rheingans, Prof. Dr. Mirko Kovac, Prof. Dr. Antonia Neels, Prof. Dr. Robert Zboray, Dr. Christian Leinenbach, Dr. Hossein Heydarinouri, Christian Affolter, Dr. Francesco Taddei, Prof. Dr. Pietro Lura, Dr. Mateusz Wyrzykowski, Dr. Rishabh Shukla, Dr. Michael Stuer, Dr. Fan Fu and Dr. Flavia Trifa at Empa for their poster presentations and lab tour. A big thank you also goes out to Viola Bhattarai and Franziska Kiss, who organized this visit with us.
