The Institute of Social and Preventive Medicine (ISPM) at the University of Bern provides undergraduate and postgraduate education and carries out interdisciplinary research in the fields of social and behavioural health, clinical epidemiology and biostatistics, and international and environmental health. Eminent speakers from Switzerland and abroad give presentations at lectures where researchers from other institutes and members of the public are welcome to attend.

To learn more about ISPM and public health, read about our vision and mission, sign up for a news subscription, take a look at our events overview, watch lecture podcasts, apply for our job openings, search for staff members in our team list and follow these quicklinks to related sites.

About us

Studies

The University of Bern’s Institute of Social and Preventive Medicine (ISPM) contributes to study programs in Human Medicine (Bachelor’s and Master’s) and Biomedical Sciences (Master’s).

Research

The ISPM consists of 14 research groups that engage in a broad range of topics related to health and disease across the lifespan, in communities, in the environment, and data science.

The ISPM consists of 14 research groups that engage in a broad range of topics.

Continuing education

ISPM Bern provides a wide range of postgraduate education to train students to become excellent public health researchers and to work as public health specialists.

ISPM Bern provides a wide range of postgraduate education.

About us

ISPM is dedicated to preserving and improving the health of the population – from local to planetary levels by educating students, professionals, and the public, conducting high-quality researchc putting research results into practice.
