Research Groups and Themes

Research Group: Mental Health

The Mental Health Research Group investigates (1) the epidemiology of mental illness, (2) the epidemiology of suicide, (3) interactions between mental illness and physical diseases, (4) the provision of mental health care, and (5) the influence of mental illness on the treatment of physical comorbidities. Most of our research is conducted in Sub-Saharan Africa, with a particular focus on South Africa.
