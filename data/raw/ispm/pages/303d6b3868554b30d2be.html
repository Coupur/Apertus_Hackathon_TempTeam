Research Groups and Themes

Research Group: Cancer

The Cancer Research Group conducts research projects on

  • Incidence and risk factors for developing cancer in children and adults living with HIV in low-, middle- and high-income countries.
  • Provision of health care and survival among HIV-positive patients with cancer in low-, middle- and high-income countries.
  • Cervical cancer prevention among women living with HIV
