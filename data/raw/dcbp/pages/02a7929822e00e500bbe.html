CV

  • 1971:  Born in Koblenz, Germany
  • 1991 – 1997:  Studies in Chemistry at Bonn University (Germany)
  • 1997 – 2000:  PhD student in the surface science group of Prof. K. Wandelt at the Institute of Physical and Theoretical Chemistry, Bonn
  • 2000-2001:  Postdoc at the MESA+ Research Institute in the solid state physics group of Prof. Dr. Bene Poelsema, University of Twente, Enschede, The Netherlands (Feodor Lynen fellowship of the Alexander-von-Humboldt foundation)
  • 2001 – 2007:  Project leader (habilitand) at the Institute of Physical and Theoretical Chemistry, Bonn (Germany)
  • 2007 – 2008:  Development of the plating chemistry for the 32- and 22-nm Cu technology at BASF SE Ludwigshafen (Germany)
  • 10/2008 – 03/2019:  Lecturer at the University of Bern and head of the Interfacial Electrochemistry group
  • Since 04/2019:  Associate professor (Assoziierter Professor) at the University of Bern and head of the Interfacial Electrochemistry group

     

Research

  • Atomic scale characterization of electrified solid/liquid interfaces by means of in situ scanning tunneling microscopy (STM) and scanning force microscopy (AFM).
  • Additive-assisted Cu electroplating for industrial applications (Damascene process, TSV technology); synthesis of polymer additives for Cu electroplating; laser ionization mass spectrometry (LIMS) depth profiling.  
  • Additive-assisted Sn (or Sn alloy) electroplating for solder bumping.
  • Electrochemical reduction of CO2 from aqueous and non-aqueous electrolyte solutions (power to gas / power to liquid); cluster synthesis; DOSY-NMR; gas chromatography (GC); ion chromatography (IC). 
  • Mass transport phenomena in ionic liquids (ILs).

Humidity Switches Molecular Diode Off and On

The tiny diode works by means of an asymmetry caused by water molecules at the molecular junction interface. At around 60% humidity, water molecules lump together at tip side of the molecular layer and cause an imbalance between the energy levels on both sides. This strongly limits the flow of electrons. Under dry circumstances, the molecule’s symmetry doesn’t break and the diode behavior disappears.

04.12.2017 Medienmitteilung UniBE
