Analytische Chemie

Analytische Chemie

Julien Furrer

We develop and apply liquid-phase and HRMAS NMR spectroscopy methods in various projects (chemistry, medical applications, material sciences).

Assoz. Prof. Dr. Julien Furrer

Analytische Chemie

Simon Grabowsky

Our group works in the field of chemical-bonding analysis and develops quantum-crystallographic techniques for the modeling of electron-density distributions.

PD Dr. Simon Grabowsky

Analytische Chemie

Stefan Schürch

Our focus is on the application of mass spectrometric techniques for qualitative and quantitative investigation of compounds in complex biological environments.

Assoz. Prof. Dr. Stefan Schürch

Analytische Chemie

Sönke Szidat

We perform radiocarbon dating. Our work focuses on (but is not restricted to) air quality studies, climate research, environmental radioactivity and archaeology.

Assoz. Prof. Dr. Sönke Szidat

Sadig Aghazada

Ass. Prof. Dr. Sadig Aghazada

Anorganische Chemie

Martin Albrecht

We employ creative ligand design to tailor the reactivity of transition metals, especially for homogeneous catalysis and the activation of poorly reactive bonds.

Prof. Dr. Martin Albrecht

Biochemie

Biochemie

Sebastian Leidel

We study the in vivo role of chemical tRNA modifications and their connection to cellular quality control, development, and disease.

Prof. Dr. Sebastian Leidel

Biochemie

Oliver Mühlemann

We are interested in post-transcriptional gene regulation and mRNA turnover, and focus on translation-coupled mRNA surveillance pathways.

Prof. Dr. Oliver Mühlemann

Biochemie

Norbert Polacek

We study structural and regulatory roles of ncRNAs in ribosome biology, translation control, and cancer cell proliferation.
 

Prof. Dr. Norbert Polacek

Biochemie

Carlos Ros

We study virus entry mechanisms, uncovering how molecular interactions between viruses and host cells drive infection and disease.

PD Dr. Carlos Ros

Biochemie

Christoph von Ballmoos

Prof. Dr. Christoph von Ballmoos

Chemiedidaktik

Prof. Dr. Matthias Arenz

Physikalische Chemie / Elektrochemie

Organische Chemie

Organische Chemie

Rebecca Buller

Prof. Dr. Rebecca Buller

Organische Chemie

Dmitry Katayev

Ass. Prof. Dr. Dmitry Katayev

Organische Chemie

Shi-Xia Liu

Our research into molecular quantum materials focuses on the synthesis of π-conjugated aromatic compounds with intrinsic optoelectronic and magnetic properties.

Assoz. Prof. Dr. Shi-Xia Liu

Organische / Pharmazeutische Chemie

Prof. Dr. Francesca Paradisi

Prof. Dr. Jean-Louis Reymond
